THE FOLKLORE

Amgu'sha (אמגושא) is the name of a lynx who used to sit on the ridge above the valley and watch the forest. She was not a good cat, and she was not a bad one. She was attentive.

They say she drank from a stream where the water ran over hot rock, and where it touched her tongue the water learned to taste like something. We named the brewery after her. We are not entirely sure she approved.

The kettle is older than the shed. The shed is older than the road. The road is older than the forest's memory, and the forest remembers everything.

— scratched into the shed door, in something that is probably paint
